Policies

Collection, cancellation, allergens and how we handle your information.

Cancellation

  • An order can be cancelled by the customer while it's still awaiting payment or verification, before the kitchen has started preparing it.
  • Once the kitchen has started preparing your order, it can no longer be cancelled.
  • Staff may cancel an order — for example an EFT payment that was never verified, or a cash order that was never accepted or collected — and will let you know if this happens.
  • An order not collected during the collection window may be marked as a no-show at the end of the day.

Your information (POPIA)

To take and fulfil your order, we store your name, mobile number, order details and — for EFT orders — a copy of your proof of payment. This is used only for order fulfilment and payment reconciliation.

Proof-of-payment images are kept for {{ proof_retention_days }} days. Order records are kept for {{ order_retention_months }} months, after which they are removed.

Your data is processed and stored on a server in the EU (Finland) on the owner's behalf, and remains subject to South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

To request that your information be deleted, {% if support_whatsapp_e164 %} message us on WhatsApp at {{ support_whatsapp_e164 }}. {% else %} contact the kitchen directly. {% endif %}

Please don't place routine orders on WhatsApp — it's for order or payment problems only. Place orders through the site so they're captured correctly.
