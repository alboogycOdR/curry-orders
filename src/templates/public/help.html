Help

How ordering, collection and payment work at Brandon's Kitchen.

Ordering & cut-off

Same-day orders close at {{ same_day_cutoff }}. You can order for any open day up to {{ preorder_days }} days ahead.

Orders are placed against {{ slot_minutes }}-minute collection slots, each with its own capacity — once a slot fills up, it stops appearing as a choice.

Collection

Collection happens during the day's collection window, currently {{ collection_window }} (this can vary by day — always check the time on your order confirmation, not this page).

The kitchen only starts preparing your order once payment is verified (EFT) or your cash order is accepted by staff — see Payment below.

Payment

EFT: pay by bank transfer using the reference on your order confirmation. Your slot is held for {{ eft_hold_minutes }} minutes — if payment isn't verified within that time, the hold is released.
